Build Walkthrough

Step-by-Step DIY Process

Follow these four core stages to flash firmware, assemble hardware, and finish your custom acrylic keychain.

STAGE 01

Graphic UI & Animation Conversion

Design a 240×240 pixel Spotify player artwork using Photoshop or Canva. Convert your video loop into a sequence of JPG frames or an MJPEG stream using ffmpeg so the ESP32 microcontroller can decode frames in real time from memory.

ffmpeg -i spotify_loop.mp4 -vf scale=240:240 -q:v 3 frame_%04d.jpg
STAGE 02

Arduino Firmware Flashing

Open Arduino IDE, select board ESP32C3 Dev Module, install libraries TFT_eSPI and TJpg_Decoder. Configure your pin definitions in User_Setup.h to match the ST7789 display.

#define ST7789_DRIVER
#define TFT_WIDTH 240
#define TFT_HEIGHT 240
STAGE 03

Soldering & Circuit Wiring

Solder short silicon flexible wires between the ESP32-C3 SPI headers, ST7789 display module, TP4056 charger board, and power switch. Tuck the 300mAh LiPo battery neatly behind the screen.

STAGE 04

3D Case Assembly & UV Resin Lens

Insert hardware into the custom 3D printed matte enclosure. Place the laser-cut clear acrylic lens over the LCD window. Optional: apply a drop of UV resin along the lens rim and cure under UV light for a glass-like finish.
